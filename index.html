Ninth Circle

An album log for you and the people you follow.

Pull a record from your Spotify or Apple Music history, rate it out of five in half stars, keep one favourite track, and see what your friends have been hearing. Mobile first, near-black, and the sleeve art is the only colour on the page.

Visit ninthcircle.xyz ↗

  • Careful with accounts.OAuth 2.0 with PKCE and Sign in with Apple, tokens encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and no auto-linking on unverified emails, which blocks account takeover.
  • Self-hosted, properly.A multi-stage Docker image running as non-root behind Caddy on a Hetzner VPS. GitHub Actions builds to GHCR and deploys over SSH.

02Data science · Sept 2026Live demo

wrestlELO

Elo ratings for twelve seasons of college wrestling.

It rebuilds every NCAA Division I bout from 2013/14 to 2024/25 out of a messy public scrape, rates each one with a tuned Elo model, and lets you browse any wrestler's career and the five bouts that mattered most.

Browse the ratings ↗
  • Scored once, at the end.Tuned on 2015–21, then tested on 2022–25: 76.8% accuracy over 90,434 bouts, and a log loss of 0.472 against 0.693 for a coin flip.
  • Honest probabilities.Expected calibration error of 0.0167 on the test seasons. No probability bin is off by more than 2.5 points.
  • Brackets, rebuilt.The raw rows ran backwards. Reversing them took “the champion's last bout is against the runner-up” from 1.7% to 89.7%.
  • What the tuning found.No offseason reversion, margin-of-victory multipliers, and K factors far above the textbook (120 and 80, not 40 and 20).

The math

Every bout runs four lines of arithmetic. The values are the tuned ones, chosen on 2015–21 and only then scored on 2022–25. ShowHide

  1. 1Predict

    Ea=11+10(Rb−Ra)/400

    Wrestler a's chance of beating b, from their ratings R. College wrestling has no draws, so this is a straight win probability. A 400-point edge is 10-to-1 odds.

  2. 2Weigh the result

    Ka=k(na)·M·2.20.001ΔR+2.2

    How far this bout moves the rating. k is 120 for a wrestler's first 8 bouts and 80 after. M rewards dominance: decision 1, overtime decision 0.7, major 1.33, tech fall 1.67, fall 2. The last factor, with ΔR = winner minus loser, damps a favourite's win and amplifies an upset, the same correction FiveThirtyEight's NFL Elo uses.

  3. 3Update

    Ra′=Ra+Ka(Sa−Ea)

    S is 1 for a win and 0 for a loss: you move by how surprising the result was. Each wrestler uses their own K, so a newcomer moves further than the veteran they beat.

    From the scoreboard: if Red wins by , Red gains +0 and Green loses 0, both past their first 8 bouts.

  4. 4Batch and carry over

    R←μ+λ(R−μ)

    Between seasons, ratings can shrink toward the mean μ = 1500. Tuning chose λ = 1, no shrinkage at all. Within a tournament round, every bout is predicted from the ratings as they stood before the round and the changes land together, so the order bouts were logged in can't matter.

Zob

A fair casino, a random walk, and a judo match that keeps score.

Zob has $1,000 and bets Banker at a baccarat table built to be exactly fair. Every hand moves the match at the top of this page: a Banker win pushes it right, a Player win pushes it left. With no edge either way it wanders, and when it finds its way back to the middle after 5,000 hands, they bow and start again. The idea for this was generally the most convoluted random walk model possible.

04Research · Cornell Deep Learning · Spring 2026

              
ResNet walked so CNNs could jog

              
How much model do you need when you barely have data?

              
I reproduced Brigato & Iocchi (2020), training plain CNNs at three levels of complexity and a ResNet-20 on class-balanced CIFAR-10 subsets from 10 to 1,280 images per class. Then I pushed further, into Fashion-MNIST, SVHN, EfficientNet-B0, and focal and label-smoothing losses.
